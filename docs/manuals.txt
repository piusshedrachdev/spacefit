# Manuals

**Audience:** Customers using products purchased through SpaceFit  
**Visibility:** Public  
**Status:** Content outline — no live product manuals are currently stored on the site

## Purpose

Manuals help customers set up, use, maintain, and safely handle the products they buy. A manual should be easy to follow without requiring technical expertise.

## Recommended manual topics

- Product assembly and setup
- Unpacking and inventory check
- Safe lifting and handling
- Materials, finishes, and care requirements
- Cleaning and maintenance schedules
- Storage and long-term care
- Troubleshooting common issues
- Warranty-related care requirements
- When to contact SpaceFit or the seller

## Manual structure

Every product manual should use the following sections where applicable:

1. **Product overview**
2. **What is included**
3. **Safety information**
4. **Assembly or setup**
5. **Care and cleaning**
6. **Maintenance schedule**
7. **Troubleshooting**
8. **Warranty or support**
9. **Product version and review date**

## Writing rules

- Put safety warnings before installation or use instructions.
- Use numbered steps and call out required tools.
- Avoid vague instructions such as “handle carefully”; explain the safe action.
- Separate product-specific instructions from general care advice.
- Include the product name and version on every manual.
- Link to the relevant return or warranty policy without changing its wording.
- Store the same approved version for sellers, support, and customers.
